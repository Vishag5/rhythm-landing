Starting is the hard part

You open your to-do list and nothing moves. The task is not hard. Starting it is. A timer does not help when you cannot begin, and a to-do app only shows you the pile.

How it works

  1. Get it out of your head. Brain dump everything on your mind.
  2. Pick one task and get a tiny first step. Something you can do in a few minutes, like "open the tax folder and find last year's form".
  3. Start the timer. A Pomodoro runs while you work.
  4. Work next to someone. Join a live body-doubling room where others quietly work too.
