Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. Plain-language summary of how Rhythm handles information.

This website

This site does not use cookies or analytics. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and we use them only to reply or to tell you when early access opens.

The Rhythm app

  • Account: email, name and sign-in details (via Firebase and Google sign-in).
  • Content you enter: tasks, brain dumps and reflections. We treat these as sensitive and do not sell them or use them for ads.
  • Usage data: product analytics (Amplitude) help us improve the app.
  • AI suggestions: the task text you submit is sent to our server to generate a tiny step.
  • Notifications: only if you allow them.

Payments

Payments are handled by our payment provider, which acts as seller of record. We do not see or store your card number.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete your data at any time by emailing info.vishag@gmail.com.

Not medical advice

Rhythm is a productivity tool. It does not diagnose, treat or replace professional care.
